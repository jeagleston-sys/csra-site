Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 1, 2026 · CSRA Precision Imaging, LLC · Augusta, GA

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a quote request through our contact form, we collect the information you voluntarily provide, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Property location or address
  • Service type requested
  • Project details you choose to share

We do not collect payment information through our website. Payments are processed through third-party providers (Stripe, PayPal, or Zelle) subject to their own privacy policies.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information you submit is used solely to:

  • Respond to your quote request or inquiry
  • Schedule and coordinate flight services
  • Communicate about your project
  • Send your deliverables (reports, imagery, data)

We do not use your information for marketing to third parties, and we do not add you to mailing lists without your explicit consent.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Our contact form uses Formspree to route submissions to our inbox. Formspree's privacy policy governs how they handle form data.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

4. Analytics

Our website uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. This service collects anonymous, aggregated data such as page views and general visitor counts. It does not use cookies and does not collect personally identifiable information. No individual user tracking occurs.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect the information you share with us. Quote request data is transmitted securely through Formspree's encrypted service and delivered to a secured business email account. We retain inquiry information only as long as necessary to complete your service engagement.

6. Your Rights

You may request that we delete any personally identifiable information you have submitted to us by contacting us directly. We will honor deletion requests promptly.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
