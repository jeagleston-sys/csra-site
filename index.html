What We Do

Drone Services for Every Client

We primarily serve commercial clients — roofing contractors, insurance adjusters, and facility managers — and also offer thermal wildlife recovery and landowner services across the CSRA.

Construction Progress & 3D Mapping

Periodic overflights that track a project from groundbreaking to completion — orthomosaic maps, 3D models, and volumetric data for owners, lenders, and project managers. Plus broader B2B aerial imaging for real estate, solar, and property management.

Thermal Roof Moisture Assessments

RGB and radiometric thermal scans that reveal moisture intrusion, heat loss, and hidden damage — delivered as insurance- and adjuster-ready reports within 48–72 hours of completed flight, weather permitting.

Post-Hunt Wildlife Recovery

Lost the blood trail? Our thermal drone locates downed deer and game fast — day or night, through heavy brush, before the meat is lost.
