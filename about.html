Our Story

Built for the CSRA

CSRA Precision Imaging, LLC is a locally owned commercial drone company serving the Central Savannah River Area. We work with property owners, roofing contractors, insurance professionals, hunters, and landowners throughout the Central Savannah River Area on both sides of the Georgia–South Carolina line.

Our FAA Part 107 certified pilot operates the enterprise-grade DJI Matrice 4TD, equipped with a 4K RGB camera and a 640×512 radiometric infrared sensor. Every flight captures both color and thermal data in a single pass, so adding thermal to a roof moisture assessment never requires a second visit.

Our pilot is also an ITC Level I sUAS Certified Infrared Thermographer — formal training in thermal image interpretation that goes well beyond simply flying a drone with a thermal camera attached. This means moisture and heat-loss findings are read with proper thermographic methodology, not guesswork.

The Matrice 4TD is DJI's flagship commercial imaging platform — not a recreational drone. Radiometric thermal embeds an actual temperature value in every pixel, enabling accurate moisture mapping and heat-loss analysis, while photogrammetry enables orthomosaic maps and 3D models for construction and mapping projects that require them.
