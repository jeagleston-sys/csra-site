Thermal Roof Moisture Assessments

We fly systematic grid patterns over residential and commercial rooftops, capturing every surface with the Matrice 4TD's dual sensors. RGB documents existing conditions; radiometric thermal reveals moisture intrusion, heat loss, and substrate damage invisible to the naked eye — all in a single flight pass, read and interpreted using formal thermographic methodology.

  • High-resolution aerial imagery of the full roof surface
