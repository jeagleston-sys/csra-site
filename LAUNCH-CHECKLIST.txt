Before you start, make sure you have:
  ✅ This folder (csra-precision-imaging) on your computer
  ✅ A phone or email address handy for account sign-ups


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 1 — Update your phone number (2 minutes)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The site currently says [Your Phone Number] in a few places.
Here's how to fix that:

  1. Open the csra-precision-imaging folder on your computer.

  2. Right-click the file called  index.html
     → Choose "Open with" → Notepad (Windows) or TextEdit (Mac)

  3. Press Ctrl+H (Windows) or Cmd+Option+F (Mac)
     This opens Find & Replace.

  4. In the "Find" box type:    [Your Phone Number]
     In the "Replace" box type: your actual phone number
     Click "Replace All"

  5. Save the file (Ctrl+S or Cmd+S), then close it.

  6. Repeat steps 2-5 for these files:
       services.html
       gallery.html
       about.html
       contact.html

  ✅ Done when: all 5 files have your real phone number.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 2 — Set up your contact form (5 minutes)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

This makes the quote form on your Contact page actually send
you an email when someone fills it out.

  1. Go to:  https://formspree.io
     Click "Get Started" and create a free account.
     (Use your business email address.)

  2. Once logged in, click "+ New Form"
     Name it something like "CSRA Quote Form"
     Click "Create Form"

  3. You'll see a line that looks like this:
       action="https://formspree.io/f/xpwzabcd"
     The part after the last slash is your Form ID.
     It's 8 characters — something like  xpwzabcd
     Copy it.

  4. Open  contact.html  in Notepad/TextEdit (same as Step 1).

  5. Press Ctrl+H (Windows) or Cmd+Option+F (Mac)
     In the "Find" box type:    YOUR_FORM_ID
     In the "Replace" box type: your actual ID from step 3
                                 (e.g.  xpwzabcd)
     Click "Replace All"

  6. Save and close contact.html.

  7. Back in Formspree, go to Settings → Notifications
     Make sure your email address is listed so you get
     emailed every time someone submits the form.

  ✅ Done when: YOUR_FORM_ID has been replaced with real letters.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 3 — Deploy to Vercel (5 minutes)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  1. Go to:  https://vercel.com
     Click "Sign Up" → sign up with your email.
     (Free account — no credit card needed.)

  2. In a NEW browser tab, go to:  https://vercel.com/drop

  3. On your computer, find the csra-precision-imaging FOLDER
     (not a file inside it — the whole folder).

  4. Drag the entire  csra-precision-imaging  folder into
     the browser window at vercel.com/drop.

  5. It will ask you to pick a project name.
     Type something like:  csra-precision-imaging
     Click "Deploy"

  6. Wait about 30 seconds. Vercel will show you a green
     checkmark and a link like:
       csra-precision-imaging.vercel.app

  7. Click that link. Your site is live on the internet! 🎉

  ✅ Done when: you can see your website at a .vercel.app address.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 4 — Test your contact form (2 minutes)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  1. Go to your live site and click "Get a Quote"

  2. Fill out the form with your own name and email, then
     submit it.

  3. Check your email inbox — you should receive a notification
     from Formspree within a minute or two.

  4. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder.
     If it's still not working, double-check that you replaced
     YOUR_FORM_ID correctly in Step 2.

  ✅ Done when: you receive the test email.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 5 — Connect your real domain (optional, ~10 min)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Right now your site is at something.vercel.app.
To use csraprecisionimaging.com instead:

  1. Buy your domain at Namecheap (namecheap.com) if you
     haven't yet. Search for csraprecisionimaging.com
     and check it out. (~$12/year)

  2. In your Vercel dashboard, click your project →
     "Settings" → "Domains" → "Add Domain"
     Type: csraprecisionimaging.com → click Add

  3. Vercel will show you two DNS records to add.
     Copy them — you'll need them in the next step.

  4. Log in to Namecheap → click "Manage" on your domain →
     click "Advanced DNS" → paste in the records Vercel gave you.
     (If you're stuck, Namecheap has live chat support.)

  5. Wait 10-30 minutes. Vercel checks automatically and
     turns on your HTTPS padlock for free.

  ✅ Done when: your site loads at csraprecisionimaging.com
     with a padlock in the browser.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 6 — How to update the site later
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Made a change? (Added a photo, fixed a typo, updated pricing)

  1. Edit the file on your computer (same Notepad/TextEdit
     method as above).

  2. Go to  https://vercel.com/drop  again.

  3. Drag the whole  csra-precision-imaging  folder in again.

  4. Select your existing project name from the dropdown
     (don't create a new one).

  5. Click Deploy. Live in ~30 seconds.

That's it — no Git, no command line, nothing complicated.
